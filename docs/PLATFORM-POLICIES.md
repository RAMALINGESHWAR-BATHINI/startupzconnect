# StartupzConnect — Platform Policies
**Version 1.0 | Effective from launch**

---

## 1. Who We Are

StartupzConnect is a verified startup networking platform connecting startups, investors, working professionals, and students. We operate a physical inspection and community review system to ensure every profile on our platform reflects reality.

---

## 2. User Eligibility & Registration

### 2.1 Who Can Register
Any individual aged 18 or above, or any legally registered entity, may create an account under one of the following roles:

| Role | Who It's For | Key Permissions |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Student** | Currently enrolled in any educational institution | Browse, apply to internships/jobs, follow startups, leave reviews after verified visits |
| **Working Professional** | Employed or freelancing with verifiable work history | All student permissions + apply for full-time roles + offer collaboration services |
| **Startup (Independent)** | Self-funded or bootstrapped company with at least one founder | Post jobs, raise funds, collaborate, showcase, apply for inspection |
| **Startup (Institutional)** | Incubated, accelerated, or spun out of an institution | All startup permissions + institution badge + expedited inspection |
| **Startup (Joint Venture)** | Co-built between two or more entities | All startup permissions + both founding entities must be individually verified |
| **Investor** | Individual or fund investing in early-stage companies | View verified profiles, express investment interest, access deal flow (paid tier) |
| **Other** | Researcher, mentor, journalist, government body, accelerator | Read-only access by default; specific permissions granted case by case |

### 2.2 One Account Per Entity
Each individual or company may maintain only one account. Duplicate accounts are permanently banned without refund of any fees paid.

### 2.3 Accurate Information Required
All information provided at registration must be true and current. Misrepresentation — including false team sizes, fake addresses, fabricated revenue numbers, or impersonating another company — results in immediate removal and may be reported to relevant authorities.

## 3. Startup Registration Requirements

### 3.1 Mandatory Information (All Startups)
- Legal company name and any trade names
- Company registration number (CIN, LLPIN, or equivalent)
- Registered address and operational address (may differ)
- Founding date
- Startup stage: Idea / MVP Built / Pre-Seed / Seed / Series A / Series B+ / Bootstrapped-Profitable
- Origin type: Independent / Institutional (with institution name) / Joint Venture
- Domain / industry
- Number of current full-time employees (verified during inspection)
- Founder names and LinkedIn profiles

### 3.2 Supporting Documents
At least one of the following must be submitted for profile activation:
- Certificate of Incorporation
- GST Registration Certificate
- DPIIT Startup India recognition certificate
- Shop and Establishment Act registration
- Any state-level startup recognition document

Documents are reviewed by our team within 48 hours. Profiles without verified documents remain in "Pending" status and are not visible to investors or featured in search.

### 3.3 Institutional Startups — Additional Requirements
- Name of the incubator / accelerator / institution
- Proof of incubation (offer letter, cohort certificate, or institution letter)
- Incubation period (start and expected end date)
- Equity or grant amount received from institution (optional but builds investor trust)

## 4. Field Inspection Policy

### 4.1 What Inspection Is
A physical visit by a trained StartupzConnect Field Agent to your operational workplace. The purpose is to verify that your startup exists as described, and to produce an independent report that investors and job seekers can trust.

### 4.2 What Is Inspected
The agent will document and verify:
- Physical address matches registered address
- Office exterior and interior photographs (geo-tagged, timestamped)
- Actual headcount present on the day of visit
- Individual photographs of team members (with consent)
- Group team photograph
- Workspace and infrastructure (computers, equipment, working environment)
- Product or work-in-progress demonstration (live demo, prototype, or screenshots)
- Any certificates or legal documents displayed on premises
- General culture and operational vibe (agent's qualitative assessment)

### 4.3 Inspection Scheduling
- Inspection must be requested by the startup (not imposed)
- Startup selects a 2-hour visit window from available slots
- Agent arrives within that window
- Startup may reschedule once with 24 hours' notice; a second reschedule forfeits the inspection fee

### 4.4 Inspection Scoring
The agent submits a structured report scoring the startup 1–10 on:
- Legitimacy (does everything check out?)
- Team quality (professional, capable-looking team?)
- Product maturity (how real and developed is the product?)
- Workspace quality (is this a real working environment?)
- Growth momentum (is there visible activity and progress?)
- Overall impression

The score is reviewed by StartupzConnect's internal team before being published. The startup is shown the full report before it goes live and may request corrections for factual errors only (not subjective scores).

### 4.5 Inspection Fees
| Tier | Includes | Fee (Launch Pricing) |
|------|----------|----------------------|
| Basic Inspection | Visit + report + badge | ₹1,000 |
| Premium Inspection | Visit + report + badge + full photo set + product showcase + video walkthrough | ₹3,000 |

Fees are non-refundable once the agent visit is completed. If the startup fails inspection (see 4.7), a partial refund of 40% is issued.

### 4.6 Badge Tiers
| Badge | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| 🔵 Registered | Documents submitted and verified |
| 🟢 Field Verified | Agent inspection completed and passed |
| 🌟 Trusted | Field Verified + minimum 5 community reviews averaging 4.0+ |
| 💎 Elite | Trusted + active funding round or confirmed investment on platform |

Badges display the date of last inspection. Field Verified and above badges expire after 18 months and require re-inspection to renew.

### 4.7 Failed Inspections
A startup fails inspection if:
- The address is incorrect or the company is not present at the stated location
- The team headcount is less than 50% of what was declared
- The product does not exist in any form
- The founders or key team members are not present or refuse the visit
- The agent identifies clear misrepresentation of any registered information

Failed inspections result in: profile flagged as "Inspection Failed", suspension from investor-facing features for 90 days, and the startup may re-apply for a new inspection after 90 days at full fee.

Repeated failures (2 or more) result in permanent removal from the platform.

## 5. Investor Policy

### 5.1 Investor Registration Requirements
Any user may express investment interest as a basic user. To receive the Verified Investor badge and access premium deal flow features, the following must be submitted:

**Individual Investors:**
- Full legal name and PAN
- Self-declaration of investable net worth bracket (₹25L–₹1Cr / ₹1Cr–₹5Cr / ₹5Cr+ / ₹25Cr+)
- At least one of: SEBI Angel Fund registration, AIF Category I/II/III registration, or a portfolio reference (name of startup invested in, investment year, amount bracket)
- LinkedIn profile with verifiable professional history

**Institutional Investors / VCs / Family Offices:**
- Fund name and legal registration
- SEBI registration number (AIF or PMS)
- Fund size declaration
- Investment thesis: preferred stages, sectors, ticket size range
- Point of contact name and designation

### 5.2 What Verified Investors Can Do
- View full startup inspection reports and photos
- Access "Deal Flow" — curated list of startups raising funds, filtered by stage, domain, and health score
- Send direct messages to startup founders
- Mark investment interest (visible to the startup, not publicly displayed)
- Access aggregated platform insights (domain trends, funding activity)

### 5.3 Investment Conduct
- Investors may not solicit startups outside the platform after initial contact is made through it
- Investors may not misrepresent their fund size, ticket size, or investment intent
- Fake investment interest expressed to collect startup information is grounds for permanent ban
- StartupzConnect does not facilitate or guarantee any investment transaction. We are a discovery and trust platform, not a broker or SEBI-registered intermediary.

## 6. Community Review Policy

### 6.1 Who Can Leave a Review
Only users who have had a verified interaction with the startup may leave a review:
- Job applicants who attended an in-person or video interview
- Employees (current or former) who worked at the startup for at least 30 days
- Investors who had at least one formal meeting with the startup
- Visitors who checked in at the startup's office via the platform's QR check-in system
- Collaborators who completed a collaboration engagement lasting at least 2 weeks

### 6.2 What Can Be Reviewed
- Work culture and environment (1–5 stars)
- Founder transparency and communication (1–5 stars)
- Team professionalism (1–5 stars)
- Product and vision clarity (1–5 stars)
- Overall experience (1–5 stars)
- Written review (minimum 50 words, maximum 500 words)

### 6.3 Review Rules
- Reviews must be based on personal, first-hand experience only
- Reviews may not contain: personal insults, discriminatory language, competitor promotion, unverifiable legal allegations, or private personal information about individuals
- Reviewers may choose to be anonymous. Anonymous reviews are still verified by the platform — the startup cannot see who wrote it, but we can
- Reviews cannot be purchased, incentivised, or coerced by any party
- Startups may flag a review for investigation. Flagging does not remove the review. If investigation confirms a policy violation, the review is removed
- Startups may post one public response to each review

### 6.4 Review Manipulation
Any startup found to be: soliciting fake positive reviews, pressuring users to remove negative reviews, or submitting fake reviews about competitor startups will be permanently removed from the platform and the review manipulation will be publicly noted on their profile for 12 months before removal.

## 7. Job Postings Policy

### 7.1 Who Can Post Jobs
Only startups with at least Registered badge status may post jobs. Unverified profiles cannot post job listings.

### 7.2 Job Posting Requirements
Each listing must include:
- Accurate job title (no misleading titles like "CEO" for an intern role)
- Employment type: Full-time / Part-time / Internship / Contract / Freelance
- Location: On-site (with city) / Remote / Hybrid
- Compensation: Salary range or "Unpaid Internship" clearly stated. Listings that hide compensation entirely are deprioritised in search.
- Skills required
- Application process and expected response time

### 7.3 Prohibited Job Listings
- Roles that require payment from the applicant at any stage
- Multi-level marketing or commission-only roles disguised as employment
- Roles with misleading equity promises (e.g. "you'll get 10% of the company" with no vesting schedule)
- Jobs at companies currently under inspection failure suspension

### 7.4 Response Obligation
Startups that post jobs are expected to respond to all applications within 21 days — either to progress the application or formally reject it. Startups with a pattern of non-response will have their job listings deprioritised in search rankings.

## 8. Content and Conduct Policy

### 8.1 Prohibited Content (All Users)
- False or misleading information about yourself, your company, or others
- Spam: unsolicited bulk messages, connection requests sent purely for promotion
- Hate speech, harassment, or discriminatory content
- Content that violates another person's intellectual property
- Sharing of confidential information obtained through the platform (e.g. sharing a startup's inspection report with a third party)

### 8.2 Confidentiality of Inspection Reports
Full inspection reports — including agent notes and photos — are visible only to the startup and to Verified Investors. A summary and badge are visible publicly. Startups may choose to make their full report public, but it is private by default.

## 9. Data and Privacy

### 9.1 What We Collect
- Registration information (name, email, phone, company details)
- Documents uploaded for verification
- Photos taken during inspection (stored securely, not sold)
- Platform activity (profile views, job applications, messages)
- Check-in data (location, timestamp) for review verification

### 9.2 What We Do Not Do
- We do not sell user data to third parties
- We do not share investor financial information with startups
- We do not share individual reviewer identities with startups (for anonymous reviews)
- We do not use uploaded legal documents for any purpose other than verification

### 9.3 Data Retention
- Active account data: retained while account is active
- Inspection reports and photos: retained for 5 years after last inspection
- Deleted account data: purged within 30 days, except data required for legal or fraud investigation purposes

## 11. Enforcement and Appeals

### 11.1 Actions We May Take
Depending on severity, violations may result in: warning, content removal, feature suspension, temporary account suspension (7–90 days), or permanent ban.

### 11.2 Appeals
Any enforcement action except permanent ban may be appealed within 14 days by emailing appeals@startupzconnect.com with the subject "Account Appeal — [your registered email]." We respond within 7 business days. Permanent bans may only be appealed once, within 30 days.

### 11.3 Reporting
Users may report any profile, listing, review, or message that violates these policies using the Report button on every piece of content. Reports are reviewed within 48 hours for urgent cases (harassment, fraud) and within 7 days for standard cases.

## 12. Platform Limitations and Disclaimers

StartupzConnect is a discovery and trust platform. We are not:
- A SEBI-registered investment advisor or broker
- A recruitment agency
- A legal verification authority (our inspection confirms operational reality, not legal compliance)
- A guarantor of any investment, employment, or collaboration outcome

Our inspection reports represent the agent's honest assessment at the time of the visit. We are not liable for changes in a startup's status after the inspection date.
